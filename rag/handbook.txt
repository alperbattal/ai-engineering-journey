Work hours: Technicians work 8 am to 5 pm, Monday to Friday, plus one Saturday shift from 9 am to 2 pm every four weeks.

On-call duty: Each technician is on call for after-hours emergencies one week per month. On-call pay is $200 per week, plus regular hourly pay for each emergency job.

Company vans: Vans are for job use only. Pay for fuel with the company fuel card. Pets are not allowed in vans or at job sites.

Safety gear: Wear safety glasses and gloves on every job. Before any gas line work, shut off the gas supply and check the area with a gas detector.

Gas smell at a customer's home: Get everyone out of the house, do not use light switches, call the gas company from outside, then notify dispatch.

Paid time off: Technicians get 15 days of paid time off per year. Request time off at least two weeks in advance in the scheduling app.

Training: The company pays for up to $1,000 per year of approved courses, including plumbing license exam prep.

Customer quotes: Always give the customer a written quote on site before starting work. Any job over $2,000 needs manager approval first.

Warranty callbacks: If a customer reports a problem within 1 year of a repair, return within 2 business days at no charge.

Uniforms: Each technician gets three company shirts per year. Wear a company shirt and your ID badge on every job.
